Where to begin

Pick a thread and follow it.

You don't have to start at page one and grind forward. You pick a series, whether a whole book, a single question, or one person, and follow it the whole way through, at ten minutes a week. Each one is a clear place to begin, and together they build an archive worth keeping. Four ways in:

A whole book

One book, start to finish

A whole book of the Bible, read a little at a time until you've walked through all of it. Familiar or not — the point is reading it slowly, together.

A single idea

The thread

One idea traced from Genesis to Revelation. A question, a prayer, a word that won't stay put.

A single person

One person, up close

One person in the Bible — Paul, David, Hagar — followed through their story and their words, day by day.

A single passage

One text, up close

One passage, taken a few lines at a time, until you really see what it says — not just what you've always heard.
